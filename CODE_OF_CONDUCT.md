# NoteNest Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in NoteNest to be welcoming, safe, respectful, and focused on constructive open-source collaboration. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to treat other people with dignity regardless of experience level, background, identity, location, communication style, or technical opinion.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- Be respectful and patient in technical disagreement.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.
- Respect privacy and do not request or expose private note content, credentials, or personal information.
- Keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant to the project.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope, safety, architecture, and moderation.
- Help make the project usable for newcomers without belittling beginner questions.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or targeted humiliation.
- Hate speech, discriminatory slurs, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately sharing malware, credential-stealing code, destructive payloads, or harmful instructions disguised as a contribution.
- Spamming, impersonation, coordinated disruption, or repeated off-topic promotion.
- Pressuring contributors to reveal private notes, local files, account credentials, signing keys, or security-report details.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith safety or conduct concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review threads, project documentation spaces, and other public or private spaces where someone is representing NoteNest.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, lock, or remove contributions or conversations that violate these expectations. Depending on severity and history, maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant from project spaces.

Moderation decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid exposing sensitive information unnecessarily.

## Reporting a conduct concern

Include enough context to understand what happened, but do not forward unrelated private information. Reports should be handled discreetly and only shared with people needed to resolve the matter.

For vulnerabilities, credential exposure, or security issues, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.

## Good-faith participation

Mistakes happen. The goal of this policy is not to punish ordinary disagreement or imperfect communication; it is to keep project spaces productive and safe. A willingness to listen, correct behavior, and repair harm matters.

## Attribution

This policy is written specifically for NoteNest and is informed by common open-source community conduct principles. It is not a substitute for the terms or policies of the platforms hosting project discussions.
